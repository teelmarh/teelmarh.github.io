01 / About

Serious about systems. Curious about everything else.

I'm a full stack developer who designs, builds and manages APIs and the backend services behind them, wires them into JavaScript front ends, and designs relational and NoSQL databases. I've spent the most time in Node.js and Laravel, but I pick the tool that fits the problem. I like owning a feature end to end: API design, frontend integration, testing and deployment.

My work sits in two worlds. One is startup product teams, where things move fast and the roadmap changes on a Tuesday. The other is enterprise systems for a national regulatory agency, where a mistake has a paper trail. I'm comfortable in both, and I think each makes me better at the other.

I like to research. I like learning new things and, better still, understanding the whys behind how things work.
